Menu

Here's what to order for.

Four lanes: matcha, bubble tea, refreshers, and whatever limited-time creation the bar is dreaming up this month. Everything is made to order at the counter — ask your barista for today's picks and prices.

Lane one

Matcha, in more ways than one.

This is a matcha bar first — it's in the name. The bar whips up matcha several ways, and regulars already have their favorites: the shop has straight-up asked its followers which matcha they drink. Point to the board and ask.

All about the matcha drinks →

Lane two

Boba & bubble tea.

Bubble tea the classic way — with chewy boba pearls — plus seasonal takes that come and go. This fall it was Pumpkin Spice Boba, the order half of Kernersville was posting about.

All about boba & bubble tea →

Lane three

Refreshers.

Cold, bright, and made for a North Carolina afternoon. Local reviewers list matcha, boba tea, and refreshers as the three things this bar is known for — the refresher lane is the one to order when matcha fatigue hits.

Lane four

Limited-time specials.

The bar runs rotating specials and announces them on its own page the day they drop. Recent ones:

  • Pumpkin Spice Boba — fall's headliner, boba pearls and all.
  • S'mores creation — added to the menu for a limited run in November.
  • Pink Potion — an October limited-time pour.

Whatever is on the special board today, it's fresh — specials rotate fast here.
