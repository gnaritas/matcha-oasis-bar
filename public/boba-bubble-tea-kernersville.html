Boba & bubble tea

Chewy pearls, cold cups, kids unbothered.

Not into matcha? No problem. The bar pours boba and bubble tea the classic way, plus cold refreshers — and the kids' room is right there, so the order isn't racing against a meltdown.

The boba lane

Bubble tea done properly.

Boba pearls are the star — the bar's own fall special, Pumpkin Spice Boba, was built around "delicious chewy boba pearls" and it drew the lines. Classic milk teas and fruit-style bubble teas come made to order at the counter; ask what's on the special board today.

Local write-ups file this bar under matcha, boba tea, and refreshers — the boba lane is half the reason the downtown foot traffic keeps showing up.

“Fall just got a little sweeter… the perfect sip of fall!” — the shop on its Pumpkin Spice Boba, October 2026

Bring the kids

A dedicated kids' room, not a play corner.

The bar ships with its own room for kids to play in — reviewers singled it out. Mommy dates, book clubs, and after-school treat runs all share the space: grown-ups get the calm, the kids get the chaos they need, and everybody wins at the counter.

  • Refreshers for the not-thirsty. Bright, cold, and quick — the third lane on the board.
  • Specials that rotate. S'mores in November, Pink Potion in October, Pumpkin Spice in the fall of it — catch them while they're posted.
  • Right in downtown Kernersville. A natural stop on the way to everywhere else downtown.
