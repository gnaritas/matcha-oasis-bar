The oasis

Built by Vanessa and Michael Ortiz to slow down a busy day.

Vanessa and Michael created Matcha Oasis Bar as a place to slow down, breathe, and catch up with friends. The bar opened in downtown Kernersville in fall 2026 and made the local new-openings list almost immediately.

The shop's own motto promises exactly that — a little escape in every sip.

“A little escape in every sip.” — the shop's own tagline, @matchaoasisbar on Instagram

The space

A room for everyone.

The bar was built for real life — kids, book clubs, mom-and-pop dates, and a five-minute breather in the middle of the week.

  • Dedicated kids' room. Little ones play in their own room while grown-ups actually finish their drink.
  • Painted by a local artist. The walls carry original artwork by local artist Christina Parrish.
  • More than drinks. The space has hosted yoga sessions and live harp melodies, plus a one-month party and Veterans Day events.
  • Community first. Holiday giving drives and military-appreciation days, announced right on the shop's own page.
