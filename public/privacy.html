Privacy

The short, honest version.

This page explains what the Matcha Oasis Bar website collects and what it does with it. Last updated November 15, 2026.

What the contact form collects

When you use the form on the contact page, you send us your name, and optionally your email and phone, plus whatever you write in the message box. That's it — the form exists for one purpose, and that purpose is answering you.

Where it goes

Your message is delivered to the bar's own inbox through an email delivery service. It is read by whoever is answering at the bar. Nobody sells it, and nothing else is attached to it.

Cookies, trackers, and analytics

This site sets no first-party cookies of its own and runs no tracking scripts or analytics. One thing to know: the directions map on the contact page is provided by Google, and it may set its own cookies from Google when the map loads on your device.

Asking for your message to be removed

Email matchaoasisbar@gmail.com and say the word — if we still have what you sent, we'll delete it.

Questions

The same email works for anything on this page: matchaoasisbar@gmail.com, or use the contact form.
