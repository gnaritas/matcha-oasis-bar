Matcha drinks

Matcha, served slow.

A whole bar in downtown Kernersville built around one leaf. Vanessa and Michael opened Matcha Oasis Bar so the town would have somewhere to breathe — and the matcha is the reason people keep coming back.

Why a matcha bar

Not a coffee shop that happens to have matcha. A matcha bar.

The difference is the focus. Matcha is the headline here — the name, the niche, and the lane the bar keeps pushing. The shop posts matcha taste-offs and asks its regulars to vote on favorites, which is a pretty good sign the lineup changes and the good ones survive.

First-timers should do what the regulars do: get to the counter, ask what's pouring this week, and let the barista point you somewhere new.

“Kernersville, meet your new matcha spot.” — a local food reel filmed at the bar, October 2026

Make a day of it

Matcha with a side of community.

The bar doubles as a neighborhood living room. Recent evenings have included harp melodies from 4 to 7, a Halloween yoga session with a local studio, a one-month anniversary party, and a Thanksgiving week giving drive — bring canned goods, get a discount on your drink.

  • Events on the calendar. Yoga nights, live music, and milestone parties — announced on the shop's own Instagram.
  • Giving drives. The bar runs community collections; the fall lineup included a food-drive week.
  • The walls. Original artwork by local artist Christina Parrish, painted right into the space.

Bringing kids? There's a dedicated kids' room — here's the boba side of the visit.
